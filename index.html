03 Physics-Aware Control Extraction from Egocentric Driving Video

Kosmic PACE

Control data for
action conditioning.

PACE is an internal physics-based tool for estimating vehicle controls from logged kinematics. It supports the preparation of action-conditioned training data for our world action models.

The public demo compares its steering and pedal estimates with controls recorded by the vehicle.

Our approach

World models for
policy training.

Our work focuses on controllable generation, temporal consistency, and physical grounding.

Read our approach ↗
  1. 01

    Action-conditioned generation

    Pair observations with control inputs so rollouts can be directed by a robot or driving policy.

  2. 02

    Long-horizon modeling

    Develop autoregressive models that retain scene state, object identity, and geometry across longer sequences.

  3. 03

    Physical grounding

    Use recorded motion, controls, and dynamics when preparing training data and evaluating generated rollouts.
