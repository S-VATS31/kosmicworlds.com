About Kosmic Worlds

World models for
physical systems.

We develop action-conditioned world models for robotics and autonomous driving.

Our focus is long-horizon simulation and physical grounding: generating future observations that reflect the actions taken in a scene.

Our work

Models, data,
and validation.

We build the models and the data tools needed to train them. V1 established our video-modeling stack. V2 is being developed around explicit actions, longer rollouts, and physical consistency.

01

Kosmic Worlds V1

Completed training run

Our first video world model: a 1.6B-parameter transformer trained on 3,600 hours of video. A public demo and technical appendix document the run.

02

Kosmic Worlds V2

In development

Our next-generation world action model. Planned variants V2D for driving and V2R for robotics target actuator-level action conditioning, synthetic policy training data, and closed-loop simulation.
