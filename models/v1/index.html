Video World Model · V1

Kosmic Worlds V1

Our first video world model: a 1.6B-parameter transformer that predicts future video in a compressed latent space.

Trained on real egocentric video. Conditioned on text, images, videos, and learned latent actions.

Architecture

Video, modeled
in latent space.

V1 separates visual compression, dynamics prediction, and reconstruction. A pretrained tokenizer turns video into discrete tokens; our transformer predicts the next latent frame.

  1. 01

    Visual context

    An initial image or video, with optional text conditioning.

  2. 02

    Tokenize

    The tokenizer encoder compresses video into a 32 × 32 token grid.

  3. 03

    Predict

    A causal transformer predicts all 1,024 tokens of the next latent frame in parallel.

  4. 04

    Decode

    The tokenizer decoder reconstructs the generated tokens as RGB video.

Spatiotemporal dynamics

The model uses 32 transformer layers, factorized spatial and temporal attention, grouped-query attention, and three-dimensional rotary position embeddings. Language embeddings enter through gated cross-attention.

Learned latent actions

A separate pathway quantizes transitions between frames into 16 learned action codes. These codes can condition generation, but do not consistently correspond to controllable physical actions.

Inference

Three ways to
start a generation.

Each path feeds visual context into the same V1 dynamics model.

  1. 01

    Text

    Qwen2-1.5B-Instruct expands the prompt; FLUX.1 [schnell] generates the initial frame. CLIP ViT-L/14 encodes the original prompt for text conditioning.

  2. 02

    Image

    The image becomes the initial frame. BLIP-2 produces a caption, which CLIP encodes for language conditioning.

  3. 03

    Video

    The input video is tokenized directly. V1’s video-input path currently operates without text conditioning.

Training and configuration

The V1 training run.

Trained on approximately 3,600 hours of Ego4D video using 8x B200s. Training took approximately 125 hours, totaling 1,000 GPU-hours. The run processed 1.93 passes over the tokenized dataset.

Results and limitations

An early foundation.

V1 established an end-to-end training and inference stack. Its limitations define the next stage of development.

From V1 to V2 ↗

Short-horizon scene persistence

The strongest generations preserve layout and lighting for approximately 3–5 seconds. This varies across prompts and decoding settings; longer rollouts can blur or lose coherence.

Inconsistent action control

The latent-action pathway functions, but action codes are not reliable semantic or physical controls.

Resolution and data constraints

Native output is 256 × 256. Minimal filtering of the training videos leaves dark, repetitive, low-motion, and degraded clips in the data.
