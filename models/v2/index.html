Development priorities

What changes in V2.

  1. 01

    Physically grounded actions

    Condition generation on actuator-level actions. V2D is planned around steering wheel angle, throttle, and brake inputs. V2R is planned around joint angles, velocities, trajectories, and torques.

    Action conditioning
  2. 02

    Longer temporal context

    Train on substantially longer sequences and develop a persistent representation of the world, so earlier scene information remains available during extended rollouts.

    Long-horizon consistency
  3. 03

    Autoregression with flow matching

    Combine causal autoregressive generation with a flow-matching objective for visual refinement. This addresses the blurring and accumulated prediction errors observed in V1.

    Visual quality
  4. 04

    Stronger data preparation

    Improve filtering before tokenization, increase native training resolution, and prepare action-aligned video data for domain-specific training.

    Training data

These are development goals. V1 results are reported separately on the V1 page and in its technical appendix.

Model family

A base model.
Domain-specific training.

V2 is being developed as the shared foundation. Fine-tuned variants will adapt that foundation to the observations and control inputs of a particular domain.

Base model · In development

Kosmic Worlds V2

General world modeling with action-conditioned generation, temporal context, and physical grounding as core research priorities.

Driving variant · Planned

Kosmic Worlds V2D

A driving-specific fine-tune intended for generating synthetic training data for E2E driving policies and closed-loop simulation.

Robotics variant · Planned

Kosmic Worlds V2R

A robotics-specific fine-tune intended for action-conditioned rollouts, synthetic policy training data, and closed-loop simulation, using joint angles, velocities, trajectories, and torques.

Scalable action labeling

PACE supports
action conditioning.

Kosmic PACE is our internal physics-based control-extraction tool. It estimates steering and pedal inputs from egocentric driving video to help prepare action labels for model training.

The public demonstration compares these estimates with recorded vehicle controls.
